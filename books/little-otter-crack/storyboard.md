# Little Otter, Crack, Crack!

## 1. Little Otter, Crack, Crack!

Little Otter,

Crack, Crack!

Illustration: A rusty red-brown sea otter floats on a turquoise ripple, holding a lavender clam and waving. A blue-gray stone rests on its tummy.

Tap cue: Tap Little Otter to begin.

Result: Turn to the next page.

## 2. Look! A shell. A little shell.

Look! A shell.

A little shell.

Illustration: Little Otter scoops a closed lavender clam from the water with one paw.

Tap cue: Tap the shell. What did Otter find?

Result: One little shell!

## 3. A little stone. On my tummy!

A little stone.

On my tummy!

Illustration: Little Otter sets an oval blue-gray stone flat on its tummy while holding the closed clam near its chest.

Tap cue: Tap the stone. Where does it go?

Result: On my tummy. Ready to tap!

## 4. Shell up high. Ready, steady...

Shell up high.

Ready, steady...

Illustration: Little Otter holds the closed lavender clam with both paws just above the stone resting on its tummy.

Tap cue: Tap the shell. Here we go!

Result: Ready to tap!

## 5. Tap, tap. Crack, crack!

Tap, tap.

Crack, crack!

Illustration: Little Otter taps a closed clam against the stone on its tummy. A fine crack appears in the shell.

Tap cue: Tap the shell to crack it open.

Result: Crack! The shell is open.

## 6. An open shell! A tasty bite inside.

An open shell!

A tasty bite inside.

Illustration: Little Otter holds an open lavender clam with two creamy halves and one peach-colored morsel inside. The stone rests on its tummy.

Tap cue: Tap the open shell. What is inside?

Result: A tasty little bite!

## 7. A little bite. Yum, yum!

A little bite.

Yum, yum!

Illustration: Little Otter brings one peach-colored morsel to its mouth; the open clam in its other paw is empty.

Tap cue: Tap Little Otter. Say yum, yum!

Result: Yum, yum! A tasty bite.

## 8. Lick, lick. Clean little paws.

Lick, lick.

Clean little paws.

Illustration: Little Otter licks its two paws under its mouth, floating with an empty tummy and no shell or stone.

Tap cue: Tap the paws. Lick, lick!

Result: Clean paws. All done!

## 9. Full little tummy. Float, float. Time to rest.

Full little tummy.

Float, float.

Time to rest.

Illustration: Little Otter floats peacefully on its back with eyes closed and both paws resting on its empty tummy.

Tap cue: Tap the tummy. Take a little rest.

Result: Rest well, Little Otter.
