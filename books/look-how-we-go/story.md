# Look How We Go!

An original English transport vocabulary picture book for toddlers. No recurring character.

## 1. Look How We Go!

Look How / We Go!

Invitation: On the road, on the water, in the sky.

Tap: So many ways to go! Ready, steady, go! Which vehicles can you name?

Art: A red car with a small airplane, sailboat and train above it.

## 2. Car

Look! A car! / The car rolls along.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Vroom, vroom! A car takes us places.

Art: A red compact car with blue windows and two dark wheels.

## 3. Bus

Look! A bus! / The bus rolls along.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Beep, beep! A bus carries lots of people.

Art: A long yellow city bus with blue windows, a door and two dark wheels.

## 4. Bicycle

Look! A bicycle! / The bicycle rolls along.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Ring, ring! Pedal, pedal. Two wheels go!

Art: A green bicycle with two wheels, pedals, a saddle and handlebars.

## 5. Train

Look! A train! / The train runs on tracks.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Choo, choo! A train carries people or things.

Art: A blue locomotive pulling a red passenger carriage along a short track.

## 6. Truck

Look! A truck! / The truck rolls along.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Rumble, rumble! A truck carries big loads.

Art: An orange delivery truck with a green cargo box and dark wheels.

## 7. Boat

Look! A boat! / The boat sails on water.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Splash, splash! The wind helps this sailboat go.

Art: A teal sailboat with a cream and coral sail on a small blue ripple.

## 8. Airplane

Look! An airplane! / The airplane flies up high.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Whoosh, whoosh! An airplane has wings.

Art: A golden passenger airplane with blue windows and both wings fully visible.

## 9. Helicopter

Look! A helicopter! / The helicopter flies up high.

Invitation: Tap the picture. Say it with me!

Tap: Whirr, whirr! Spinning blades! Can you name a vehicle on the road, on water, and in the sky?

Art: A red helicopter with blue cockpit windows, a main rotor, tail rotor and landing skids.
