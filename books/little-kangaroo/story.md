# Brush, Brush, Roo!

## 1

Brush, brush, / Roo!

Roo, an orange kangaroo in teal overalls, proudly holds a red toothbrush.

Tap: Let's help Roo brush!

## 2

Crunch, munch! / Yummy, Roo!

Roo enjoys a crunchy red apple with a banana beside his feet.

Tap: Strong teeth help us munch!

## 3

No brushing? / Teeth can / turn yellow.

Roo notices yellow plaque on his unbrushed teeth in a hand mirror.

Tap: Time to care for those teeth!

## 4

Cavities can hurt. / Biting can / hurt, too.

Roo imagines a tooth with a cavity and how it could hurt to bite an apple.

Tap: Let's help keep teeth healthy!

## 5

A little paste. / Ready, Roo?

A grown-up puts a tiny smear of toothpaste on Roo's red toothbrush.

Tap: Just a little. A grown-up helps!

## 6

Brush, brush! / Front teeth.

Roo gently brushes the front of his teeth.

Tap: Small, gentle circles!

## 7

Brush, brush! / All around.

Roo brushes toward his back teeth; a small diagram shows the inner and chewing surfaces.

Tap: Outside, inside, and chewing tops!

## 8

Grown-up helps. / Spit it out!

A grown-up supports Roo as he spits toothpaste into a white basin.

Tap: Spit. Don't rinse!

## 9

Morning and night. / Keep teeth strong!

Roo smiles with his toothbrush, with sun and moon icons and favorite fruit nearby.

Tap: Brush, brush, Roo! Every day!
