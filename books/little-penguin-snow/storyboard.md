# Little Penguin, Snow Much Fun!

## 1. Little Penguin, Snow Much Fun!

Little Penguin,

Snow Much Fun!

Illustration: A little penguin in a pastel striped scarf waves on the snow.

Tap cue: Tap Little Penguin to begin.

Response: Turn to page 2.

## 2. Step, step. Here we go!

Step, step.

Here we go!

Illustration: Little Penguin lifts one foot and leaves four footprints in soft snow.

Tap cue: Tap the footprints. Step, step!

Response: Little steps in the soft, soft snow.

## 3. A snowflake! Hello, snow!

A snowflake!

Hello, snow!

Illustration: Little Penguin holds out a flipper beneath a falling snowflake.

Tap cue: Tap the snowflake. Can you catch it?

Response: Hello, little snowflake!

## 4. Roll, roll. Here we go!

Roll, roll.

Here we go!

Illustration: Little Penguin pushes a white snowball with both flippers.

Tap cue: Tap the snowball. Roll, roll!

Response: Round and round. A snowball!

## 5. Pat, pat. A snow penguin!

Pat, pat.

A snow penguin!

Illustration: Little Penguin stands beside a small two-ball snow penguin with a carrot beak and twig flippers.

Tap cue: Tap the snow penguin. Say hello!

Response: Hello, snowy friend!

## 6. Slide, slide. Here we go!

Slide, slide.

Here we go!

Illustration: Little Penguin slides on its belly over a low snow mound, feet trailing behind.

Tap cue: Tap Little Penguin. Wheee!

Response: Wheee! A soft, snowy slide.

## 7. Flap, flap. A snow angel!

Flap, flap.

A snow angel!

Illustration: Little Penguin lies on its back with flippers spread in a pale blue snow-angel impression.

Tap cue: Tap the flippers. Flap, flap!

Response: Flippers out. Flippers in. Flap, flap!

## 8. Brush, brush. Off goes the snow!

Brush, brush.

Off goes the snow!

Illustration: Little Penguin brushes loose snow from its tummy with one flipper.

Tap cue: Tap the snowy tummy. Brush, brush!

Response: Brush, brush. Ready to go!

## 9. Bye-bye, snow! Step, step. Home we go!

Bye-bye, snow!

Step, step.

Home we go!

Illustration: Little Penguin waves beside its snow penguin, with footprints leading away.

Tap cue: Tap a flipper to wave goodbye.

Response: Bye-bye, snowy friend. Snow much fun!
