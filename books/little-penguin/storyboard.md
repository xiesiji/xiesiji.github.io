# Little Penguin, Time to Tidy!

主题：玩完玩具主动收拾。9 幅独立无字插画（含封面）。

角色：深蓝小企鹅，奶油白脸与肚子、橙色喙和脚、珊瑚红围巾。道具：黄色敞口玩具盒，青绿色内壁；红积木、蓝积木、绿色小车、条纹黄球。

教育节奏：主动发现 → 一件件收好 → 检查遗漏 → 温暖庆祝。重复句：In the box!

## 1. Little Penguin, Time to Tidy!

Little Penguin,
Time to Tidy!

画面：A navy blue paper-collage penguin wearing a coral scarf waves beside a mustard yellow box filled with colorful toys.

点击提示：Tap Little Penguin to begin.

点击结果：进入下一页。

## 2. Time to play

Stack, stack!
Vroom, vroom!
Roll, roll!

画面：Little Penguin plays with two colorful blocks, a green toy car, and a striped yellow ball. An empty mustard toy box stands nearby.

点击提示：Can you say vroom, vroom? Tap to look around.

点击结果：进入下一页。

## 3. Playtime is done

Playtime is done.
My toys are on the floor.
I can put them away!

画面：Little Penguin looks at the scattered red and blue blocks, green car, and yellow ball, then points toward the empty open toy box.

点击提示：Tap the toys. Time to tidy!

点击结果：进入下一页。

## 4. One little block

One little block.
In the box!

画面：Little Penguin releases the red block directly above the open mustard toy box. The blue block, green car, and striped yellow ball remain on the floor.

点击提示：Tap to help put the red block away.

点击结果：进入下一页。

## 5. One more block

One more block.
In the box!

画面：Little Penguin releases the blue block into the toy box beside the red block already inside. The car and ball remain outside.

点击提示：Tap to help put the blue block away.

点击结果：进入下一页。

## 6. Vroom, little car

Vroom, little car.
In the box!

画面：Little Penguin lowers the green toy car into the open box with both blocks inside. Only the striped yellow ball remains on the floor.

点击提示：Tap to help park the little car.

点击结果：进入下一页。

## 7. One last toy

Look! One last toy.
In the box!

画面：Little Penguin releases the striped yellow ball above the toy box containing the red block, blue block, and green car. Nothing remains on the floor.

点击提示：Tap the ball. In it goes!

点击结果：The ball is in. All toys away!

## 8. All toys away

I look all around.
All toys away.
Ready for another day!

画面：Little Penguin looks around the clear floor with empty flippers open. Both blocks, the car, and ball are visibly stored inside the open mustard box.

点击提示：Look around with Little Penguin. Anything left?

点击结果：All clear! You put every toy away.

## 9. Hooray, Little Penguin!

When playtime is done,
toys go in the box.
Hooray, Little Penguin!

画面：Little Penguin raises a flipper for a high five beside the neatly filled toy box. The floor is clear.

点击提示：Tap a flipper for a high five!

点击结果：High five! I can tidy up, too!
